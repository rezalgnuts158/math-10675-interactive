Inside this investigation

  1. Identify quantities and define variables

    Classify quantities in the bike ride, then define variables for the bike and pool scenarios.

  2. Connect changes, rates, and initial values

    Explore reciprocal rates and use Δy = mΔx to distinguish a change from a final value. Work through the pool observations and randomized self-checks.

  3. Explore constant rates on graphs

    Move initial and final points on five linear models, reverse intervals, and examine why a zero-length interval gives an undefined ratio.

  4. Distinguish proportional changes from proportional values

    Compare two starting depths, scale a mixture, compare cylinder readings, and model remaining candle length versus length burned.
