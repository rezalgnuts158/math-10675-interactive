Module 2 · Investigations 1 & 2

Quantities & change

September 13, 2026

Identify measurable quantities, define variables, and connect a quantity’s value to its signed change. Explore graphs and reason through the tortoise-and-hare race and a walk to the car.

  • Quantities
  • Variables

Inside this investigation

  1. Identify and define

    Classify ten candidates, explain which criteria fail, and write variable definitions for a marathon and a ball above a rooftop.

  2. Separate a value from its change

    Explore temperature readings, use ΔT = Tfinal − Tinitial, and check your understanding with new practice values.

  3. Read change on a graph

    Move two points on walking, square-area, and reservoir graphs. Compare initial values, final values, and signed changes.

  4. Use the quantities in a story

    Work through the race and the walk to the car. Distinguish position, distance traveled, distance remaining, and signed lead.
